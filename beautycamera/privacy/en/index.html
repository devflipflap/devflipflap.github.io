Beauty Camera Privacy Policy

Effective: November 25, 2026 · 한국어

FLIPFLAP ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who uses the camera app "Beauty Camera" (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. No account

  • The App has no account, sign-up or login, and does not ask for your name, phone number or email.

2. Photos, videos and face data never leave your device

  • Photos and videos you take or edit are saved only in your device's photo folder (Pictures/BeautyCamera). They are not sent to our servers or anywhere else.
  • Face position and person detection for beauty effects, face stickers, ID photo backgrounds and portrait mode run only on your device, and the results are not stored or transmitted.
  • Photos you open in the photo editor are edited on your device and saved as new photos on your device.

3. Permissions

  • Camera (required): preview and taking photos and videos. Without it the App cannot work.
  • Microphone (optional): video sound and the voice shutter (say a word such as "Cheese" to take a photo). Without it, videos are recorded without sound and the voice shutter is off. While the camera screen is open, the voice shutter listens using your device's speech recognition; on-device recognition is used first, and on devices without it the speech service installed on your device (for example, Google) may process the audio under that service's policy. The App does not record, store or send your voice to us, and you can turn the voice shutter off in Settings.
  • The App does not request permission to read your photos. The photo editor opens only the photo you pick in the system photo picker.

4. Ads, usage statistics, crash reports and third-party SDKs

  • The App uses the following Google LLC services, which collect information. Photos, videos and face data are not sent to them.
  • Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform: advertising ID, IP address, device information (model, OS version, language) and ad impression/click information, for serving ads, measuring ad performance and preventing fraud. Where consent is required (such as the EU), the App asks for ad consent on first launch.
  • Firebase Analytics: app instance ID (a random identifier created at installation), device information, approximate location from the IP address (country/city level) and app usage events (app open/update/removal, screen views, capture mode, filter and beauty selections, whether an ad was shown, etc.), for improving features.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash and error logs with device state (model, OS version, memory, etc.) and the app instance ID, for fixing errors.
  • Firebase Remote Config: app instance ID and app version, to deliver settings such as ad frequency and update requirements.
  • Google Play In-App Updates: Google Play checks the app version to offer updates.
  • Retention: Analytics for the period set in Firebase (up to 14 months), Crashlytics 90 days, ad information under Google's policies.
  • Google's practices: Privacy Policy, Advertising, Firebase
  • To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

5. Sharing and international transfers

  • We do not provide information to third parties other than the Google services in section 4.
  • That information is sent to Google LLC (United States) while you use the App and may be processed in Google data centers in the United States and elsewhere.

6. Your rights

  • Uninstalling the App deletes its settings on your device (your photos stay in your photo folder).
  • For access or deletion requests regarding the information in section 4, contact us below.

7. Children

  • The App is not directed at children under 14.

8. Contact

  • Operator: FLIPFLAP
  • Email: devflipflap@gmail.com

9. Changes

  • If this policy changes, we will post the new version and effective date on this page.
